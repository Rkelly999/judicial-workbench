Engine log
Chrome's speech engine silently restarts itself after short pauses — anything said in that restart window is what usually gets dropped. Watch the log while you dictate: a run of "restart gap" warnings lining up with missed words is the tell. A low, flat input level bar during speech points to a mic/volume issue instead.
